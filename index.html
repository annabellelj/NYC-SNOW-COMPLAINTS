Less snow, far more complaints

New Yorkers filed 29,265 snow and ice complaints with 311 from Jan. 1 to Feb. 6, 2026, 5.7 times as many as in the same weeks of 2016, when Central Park got more than twice as much snow.

By Annabelle Jiang
